Real Results: What to Expect

We rotated two sample PDFs by 90°, 180° and 270° and measured the pages that came out. Rotating changes how each page is displayed, not what is on it: the page count and the text are the same afterwards. A landscape page rotated by 90° becomes a portrait page, and the other way round.

  • Text unchanged: we extracted the text of every page before and after each rotation and compared it: identical in every case.
  • Every page, every time: the rotation is applied to all pages of the file; there is no option to rotate only some of them.
  • Rotation adds up: after one 90° rotation our landscape sample was set to 90°; rotating that result by 90° again gave 180°, and by 270° gave 0° (back to how it started). A PDF whose pages were stored sideways (595 × 842 pt, set to 90°) came out upright after 270°: 842 × 595 pt, set to 0°.
