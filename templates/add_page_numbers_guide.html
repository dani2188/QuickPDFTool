How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF

Page numbers are essential for any document longer than a few pages. They make it easy for readers to navigate, reference specific sections, and keep printed copies in order. If you've ever tried to discuss a multi-page PDF with a colleague without page numbers, you'll know how frustrating it can be.

Our free online tool stamps page numbers onto every page of your PDF in seconds. No software to install, and your file is handled securely.

Step 1 – Open the Add Page Numbers Tool

Go to our Add Page Numbers tool. You'll see a file upload area where you can click to select your PDF or drag and drop it from your desktop.

Step 2 – Upload Your PDF

Select your PDF and upload it. The tool accepts any standard PDF regardless of the number of pages. Longer documents benefit most from page numbering, but even short documents are easier to reference when numbered.

Step 3 – Download the Numbered PDF

The tool adds a page number to the bottom-right corner of each page. Processing takes only a few seconds. Once done, download the updated PDF to your device. The page numbers are embedded permanently into the document.

Tips for Adding Page Numbers to PDFs

  • Prepare your document first: If your PDF has blank pages, a cover page, or sections you plan to remove, make those edits first using the Delete Pages or Merge PDF tools. Adding page numbers should be one of the last steps in your workflow.
  • Numbering after merging: When you merge multiple PDFs, the resulting document may not have consistent page numbers. Run it through the page numbering tool after merging to get a clean sequential count.
  • Professional documents: Reports, manuals, legal filings, and academic submissions always require page numbers. Add them before finalising and distributing any formal document.
  • Printing: Numbered pages make reassembling a printed document easy if the pages get mixed up.

Common Reasons to Add Page Numbers

Legal and compliance documents: Court submissions, contracts, and compliance reports often require sequential page numbering. Some jurisdictions mandate it for official filings.

Academic papers and theses: Academic institutions universally require page numbers in dissertations, reports, and research papers for citation purposes.

Business reports: A page-numbered report is much easier to present in a meeting — "turn to page 12" is far more useful than "scroll down a bit."

Instruction manuals and guides: Any technical document benefits from page numbers so users can easily find the section they need.

Scanned documents: Scanned multi-page documents often lack page numbers. Adding them after scanning makes the archive far more navigable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the page numbers placed?

The tool places page numbers at the bottom-right corner of each page. This is the most common and professional position for page numbers.

Can I start numbering from a number other than 1?

Currently the tool starts numbering from 1. If you need a different starting number, this is not supported at this time.

Will the page numbers appear when I print the PDF?

Yes. The numbers are embedded into the PDF content, so they will appear in any PDF viewer and when the document is printed.

Can I add page numbers to a password-protected PDF?

You'll need to remove the password first. Then add page numbers, and re-protect the file if needed.
