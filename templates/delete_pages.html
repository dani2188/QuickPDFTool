Delete Pages from a PDF

Remove specific pages from a PDF — blank scans, duplicate sheets, or sections you don't want to share — by typing their page numbers. The rest of the document stays exactly as it was, and you download a new PDF with just those pages taken out.

How to delete pages from a PDF

  • 1 Upload your PDF
  • 2 Enter the page numbers to remove, separated by commas (e.g. 2,5,7)
  • 3 Click Delete Pages
  • 4 Download the updated PDF

When to use this tool

Blank scanner pages: Scanners often insert a blank page between documents — delete those before sharing.

Removing sensitive sections: Drop a page containing information you don't want to include before sending a document on.

Trimming duplicates: Remove a page that was accidentally scanned or included twice.

Important considerations

Page numbers must be entered individually and separated by commas (e.g. 2,5,7) — page ranges like 2-5 aren't supported, so list each page number you want removed. Numbering starts at 1, matching the page numbers you'd see in a PDF viewer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter a range of pages, like 2-5?

Not currently — list each page number individually, separated by commas (e.g. 2,3,4,5).

Does page numbering start at 0 or 1?

Page 1 is the first page, matching what you'd see in any PDF viewer.

Will deleting pages affect the quality of the remaining pages?

No. The remaining pages are copied over unchanged — only the specified pages are removed.

Is there a limit to how many pages I can delete at once?

No, you can delete as many pages as you like in a single pass, as long as the file stays under the 20MB upload limit.
