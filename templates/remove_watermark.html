Remove Watermark from PDF Online

Automatically detects and removes the watermark text added by our own Add Watermark tool, then cleans up the PDF's internal content streams generally. Useful when you need a tidier copy of a document for reuse, sharing, or further editing.

How it works

  • 1 Upload your PDF
  • 2 Click Remove Watermark
  • 3 Download the cleaned PDF

When to use this tool

Cleaning up shared documents: Remove leftover overlay text from a document before repurposing it.

Reusing content: Strip overlays so a document reads cleanly when reused elsewhere.

Important considerations

This tool recognizes the specific watermark our own Add Watermark tool adds — by its font, size, color, and position — and removes exactly that, on top of a general cleanup of the PDF's internal content streams. It does not detect or remove watermarks added by other PDF software, since those can look completely different and there's no reliable way to recognize them automatically. It also does not perform OCR or image editing, so a watermark baked into a scanned image (part of the image pixels themselves, not separate text) won't be removed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this remove any watermark?

No, only the specific watermark added by our own Add Watermark tool. This tool recognizes that watermark's exact appearance and removes it; it doesn't detect or remove watermarks added by other PDF software, and it can't remove a watermark baked into a scanned image's pixels.

Will removing a watermark affect the rest of my document?

The tool only removes the specific watermark text it recognizes — the underlying text, images, and any other content in your document are preserved.

Can I add a watermark back afterward?

Yes — use the Add Watermark tool to stamp new text onto the cleaned file.

Is this legal to use on any document?

Only remove watermarks from documents you own or have the right to modify. Removing a watermark from copyrighted or licensed material without permission may violate its terms.

Are my files kept private?

Yes. Your uploaded file is processed and automatically deleted from our server within a few minutes.
