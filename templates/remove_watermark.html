Remove Watermark from PDF Online

Clean up a PDF's page content to strip out watermark overlays and redundant content streams. Useful when you need a tidier copy of a document for reuse, sharing, or further editing.

How it works

  • 1 Upload your PDF
  • 2 Click Remove Watermark
  • 3 Download the cleaned PDF

When to use this tool

Cleaning up shared documents: Remove leftover overlay text from a document before repurposing it.

Reusing content: Strip overlays so a document reads cleanly when reused elsewhere.

Important considerations

This tool works by cleaning and normalizing the PDF's internal content streams — it's most effective on watermarks that were added as simple text or vector overlays (including watermarks added with our own Add Watermark tool). It does not perform OCR or image editing, so a watermark baked into a scanned image (i.e. part of the image pixels themselves, not a separate overlay) won't be removed. Results can vary depending on how the original watermark was applied.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this remove any watermark?

It's most effective on text or vector overlays added as separate PDF content, like those from our Add Watermark tool. A watermark burned into a scanned image's pixels can't be removed this way.

Will removing a watermark affect the rest of my document?

The tool only cleans up overlay content — the underlying text and images in your document are preserved.

Can I add a watermark back afterward?

Yes — use the Add Watermark tool to stamp new text onto the cleaned file.

Is this legal to use on any document?

Only remove watermarks from documents you own or have the right to modify. Removing a watermark from copyrighted or licensed material without permission may violate its terms.

Are my files kept private?

Yes. Your uploaded file is processed and automatically deleted from our server within a few minutes.
