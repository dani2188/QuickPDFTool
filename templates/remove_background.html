Tips for the Best Results

  • Use a clear, well-lit subject. Sharp contrast between the subject and background gives the most accurate cut-out.
  • Simple backgrounds work best. A plain wall or solid backdrop is detected more reliably than a busy, cluttered scene.
  • Fine details like hair or fur are usually handled well, but very wispy or semi-transparent edges (glass, smoke) can be less precise.
