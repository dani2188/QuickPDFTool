How to Convert JPG to PDF

Converting JPG images to PDF is one of the most common document tasks. Whether you've scanned documents with your phone, need to combine product photos for a client, or want to turn a series of images into a presentable document, converting JPG to PDF is the solution.

A PDF is far more professional and portable than a collection of loose image files. It preserves layout, looks the same on every device, and is universally accepted by email systems, government portals, and document management tools.

Step 1 – Open the JPG to PDF Tool

Go to our JPG to PDF converter. You'll find a file upload area where you can select your image files. The tool supports JPG and JPEG formats.

Step 2 – Upload Your Images

Click to select your images or drag and drop them onto the upload area. You can select multiple images at once. The images will be converted in the order you upload them, so if the final page order matters (for example, page 1 before page 2), make sure to select them in sequence.

On Windows, you can hold Ctrl and click to select multiple files. On Mac, hold Cmd. The tool will accept all selected images and combine them into a single PDF.

Step 3 – Download Your PDF

The tool processes your images immediately and creates a PDF with one image per page. When it's ready, download the PDF to your device. The file is clean, properly formatted, and ready to share or print.

Tips for Converting JPG Images to PDF

  • Image quality matters: The PDF will use your original images as-is. If your JPGs are blurry or low-resolution, the PDF will reflect that. For best results, use images of at least 150 DPI.
  • Page order: Upload images in the order they should appear in the final PDF. If you need to change the order afterwards, use the Merge PDF tool to rearrange.
  • Reduce the final PDF size: If your images are high-resolution, the resulting PDF may be large. Use our Compress PDF tool afterwards to reduce the file size.
  • Multiple pages: You can upload as many images as you need in one session. Each image becomes one page in the final PDF.
  • Scanned documents: If you've scanned multiple pages of a form or contract as separate JPG files, this tool is the easiest way to reassemble them into a single PDF document.

Common Use Cases

Submitting scanned documents: Many government portals, banks, and HR systems require documents in PDF format. If you scanned your ID, contract, or form as images, convert them to PDF before submitting.

Creating a photo portfolio: Artists, photographers, and designers can convert a selection of JPG images into a clean, paginated PDF portfolio to share with clients.

Combining receipts or invoices: If you have photographed or scanned multiple receipts, converting them to a single PDF keeps your records organised.

Packaging product images: E-commerce sellers often need to provide product images in PDF format for catalogues or supplier documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple JPG files into one PDF?

Yes. Upload all your images at once and the tool will combine them into a single multi-page PDF document.

Does the conversion change the quality of my images?

The images are embedded into the PDF as-is. The tool does not apply additional compression to your images during conversion, so quality is preserved.

What if my images are PNG or WEBP format?

For PNG images, use our PNG to PDF tool. For other formats, convert them to JPG or PNG first, then use this tool.

Is there a limit on how many images I can convert?

There is no strict limit. However, very large numbers of high-resolution images may increase processing time. If the resulting PDF is very large, use the Compress PDF tool to reduce its size.
