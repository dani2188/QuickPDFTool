Crop PDF Online

This tool allows you to crop PDF pages by removing unwanted margins. It is perfect for improving readability, adjusting layouts, or preparing documents for printing.

Why crop PDF pages?

  • Remove white margins
  • Focus on important content
  • Improve document appearance
  • Optimize for printing

How to crop a PDF

  • 1 Upload your PDF file
  • 2 Enter crop values (top, bottom, left, right)
  • 3 Click "Crop PDF"
  • 4 Download your cropped file

Tips for best results

Start with small values and adjust gradually. Avoid cropping too much to prevent losing important content.

Frequently Asked Questions

What unit are the margin values in?

Points, not pixels — the same unit PDF pages are measured in internally (72 points = 1 inch). A standard US Letter page is 612 × 792 points, and A4 is roughly 595 × 842 points. To remove a 1-inch margin, enter 72; for half an inch, enter 36.

Does cropping delete the trimmed content permanently?

It removes the trimmed area from the visible page boundary. If you crop too aggressively and need the original back, re-upload your unmodified source file rather than trying to "un-crop" the result.

Can I crop each page differently?

No, the same top/bottom/left/right values are applied to every page in the document in one pass.

Will cropping resize or distort the page content?

No. Cropping only changes the page boundary — everything inside the new boundary stays at its original scale and position.
