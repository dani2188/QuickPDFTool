How to Convert PDF to PNG

Converting a PDF to PNG produces high-quality lossless images of each page. Unlike JPG, PNG uses lossless compression, which means there is no quality degradation — every pixel is preserved exactly. This makes PNG the preferred format when you need crisp, accurate representations of PDF pages, especially for documents with sharp text, line art, or transparent backgrounds.

PNG vs JPG – When to Choose PNG

  • PNG is lossless: No image quality is lost during compression. Text remains sharp, lines stay crisp.
  • JPG is lossy: Some quality is sacrificed for a smaller file size. Fine for photographs, but may introduce artefacts around text and sharp edges.
  • Choose PNG when: the PDF contains text, diagrams, line art, logos, or anything with sharp edges where quality must be perfect.
  • Choose JPG when: file size is a priority and the content is photographic with no sharp text edges.

Common Uses for PDF to PNG Conversion

Web design and development: PNG images can be embedded in websites and web apps. Converting PDF diagrams, charts, or page designs to PNG makes them easy to use in HTML/CSS without requiring a PDF viewer.

Presentations: Import individual PDF pages as PNG slides into PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Keynote for pixel-perfect image quality.

Documentation and wikis: Technical documentation platforms and wikis like Confluence or Notion support inline PNG images but not embedded PDFs. Converting PDF screenshots or diagrams to PNG makes them easy to embed.

Social media graphics: Convert infographic pages from a PDF to PNG to share on Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn where image posts get more visibility than document attachments.

Archiving with transparency: PNG supports transparent backgrounds, making it possible to extract diagrams or charts with non-rectangular shapes from PDFs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pages can I convert at once?

The tool converts up to 20 pages per upload. For longer documents, use the Split PDF tool to break the document into smaller parts first.

What resolution are the PNG files?

Pages are rendered at 2x resolution. A standard A4 page produces an image of approximately 1654 × 2339 pixels, which is sharp enough for both screen use and most print applications.

What is the difference between PDF to PNG and PDF to JPG?

PNG is lossless — ideal for text, diagrams, and sharp graphics. JPG is lossy but produces smaller file sizes — better for photographic content where exact pixel accuracy is less critical.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?
