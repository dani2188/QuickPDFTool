How to Sign a PDF

Add a signature to your PDF document in a few simple steps using our online PDF signer.

Step 1 – Upload your PDF

Open the Sign PDF tool and upload the PDF file you want to sign.

Step 2 – Upload your signature image

Upload a signature image (PNG, JPG, or GIF) using the signature upload box.

Step 3 – Position and resize the signature

Use drag-and-drop to move the signature on the preview, and use the corner handle to resize it.

Step 4 – Apply signature and download

Click "Apply Signature" to embed the signature into the PDF, then download the signed PDF.

Why use this tool?

  • Fast electronic signatures without installing software
  • Preview the location before saving
  • Supports PDF documents up to 10MB
