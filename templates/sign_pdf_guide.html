How to Sign a PDF Online

Adding a signature to a PDF doesn't require any software. Our free Sign PDF tool lets you upload your signature image and place it anywhere on the document — no account needed.

Step 1 – Open the Sign PDF tool

Go to the Sign PDF page. You will see two upload boxes: one for your PDF document and one for your signature image.

Step 2 – Upload your PDF

Click the PDF upload box (or drag and drop your file onto it). The file must be under 10MB. Any standard PDF document works.

Step 3 – Upload your signature image

Click the signature upload box and select your signature image. Supported formats are PNG, JPG, and GIF. For the best result, use a PNG with a transparent background so the signature blends naturally with the document.

Step 4 – Choose the page to sign

Enter the page number you want the signature to appear on. Page 1 is selected by default. Click Upload & Continue to generate a preview.

Step 5 – Position and resize the signature

A preview of the selected page will appear with your signature overlaid on it. You can:

  • Drag the signature box to move it anywhere on the page
  • Drag the red corner handle to resize the signature

Take your time to position it exactly where the signature line is on the document.

Step 6 – Apply and download

Click Apply Signature. The signature is embedded into the PDF at the position you set, and the signed file downloads automatically.

Tips for a clean signature

  • Use a PNG with a transparent background for a professional look
  • Sign on white paper with a dark pen, then photograph or scan it
  • Crop the image tightly around the signature before uploading
  • Resize the box on the preview to match the signature line on the document

Is it secure?

All uploaded files are processed on our server and automatically deleted within 10 minutes. No files are stored permanently and no account is required.
