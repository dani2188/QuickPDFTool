Convert Word to PDF Online

This tool converts a .doc or .docx file to PDF using the same rendering engine a full office suite uses, rather than a simplified text-only conversion — so fonts, images, tables, headers/footers, and page layout carry over the way they would if you'd used "Save as PDF" in a desktop word processor.

Converting to PDF is usually the right move once a document is finished: unlike a .docx file, a PDF displays identically on every device and every reader, regardless of which fonts or software the recipient has installed — nothing reflows or shifts.

How to convert Word to PDF

  • 1 Upload your Word document (.doc or .docx)
  • 2 Click Convert
  • 3 Download the PDF file

When to use this

  • Sending a finished document (resume, report, contract) so it looks identical for the recipient regardless of their software
  • Submitting a document to a portal or form that specifically requires PDF
  • Archiving a document in a format that won't shift or reflow if opened years later in different software

Important considerations

  • Complex layouts convert best when kept simple. Straightforward documents (text, standard tables, images) convert cleanly. Very elaborate layouts, embedded objects, or unusual fonts not available during conversion can occasionally render slightly differently than in the original.
  • Once it's a PDF, it's not a Word document anymore. The output isn't editable text the way the original .docx was — if you need to keep editing, keep working in Word and only convert the final version.
  • Fonts embedded in the original document generally carry over correctly, since the conversion renders the document as it actually appears rather than re-typesetting it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my formatting and fonts be preserved?

Yes, in almost all cases — this uses a real document-rendering engine rather than a simplified text extractor, so layout, fonts, tables, and images convert the way they'd look if printed from Word directly. Extremely unusual layouts or rare fonts can occasionally shift slightly.

Can I edit the PDF afterward?

Not as text the way you could in Word. If you need to add a note or signature, use Add Text to PDF or Sign PDF afterward — for substantial edits, it's easier to edit the original Word document and re-convert.

Does this work with both .doc and .docx?

Yes, both older .doc and modern .docx formats are accepted.
