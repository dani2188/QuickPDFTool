How to Resize an Image for Social Media

{% from "components/byline.html" import byline %} {{ byline(date_published, date_modified) }} {% from "components/open_tool.html" import open_tool %} {{ open_tool("/resize-image", "Open the Resize Image tool") }}

Upload a photo with the "wrong" dimensions to most platforms and it gets auto-cropped — sometimes cutting off exactly the part of the image you cared about. Resizing to the platform's actual recommended size first gives you control over how your photo appears instead of leaving it to an algorithm.

Step 1 – Open the Resize Image Tool

Go to our Resize Image tool. Upload your photo by clicking the upload area or dragging it in.

Step 2 – Pick a Preset

Choose the preset matching where you're posting — Instagram square, Instagram Story/Reels, a YouTube thumbnail, and more are all built in. Each preset targets the exact pixel dimensions that platform recommends.

Step 3 – Choose PNG or JPG

The tool page explains which format suits which kind of picture. If you choose JPG, the quality slider lets you trade detail for a smaller file: lower values give a smaller file.

Step 4 – Download Your Resized Image

Click "Resize Image" and the file downloads automatically, sized exactly to your chosen preset.

How the Resize Actually Works

Your photo is scaled down (never up, and never cropped) so it fits entirely within the target dimensions while keeping its original proportions. If your photo's shape doesn't exactly match the target — a landscape photo going into a square slot, for example — the leftover space is padded with white rather than cropping any part of your photo away. This means the entire original image is always preserved somewhere in the output, even if that means white bars on the sides or top and bottom.
