How to Add a Watermark to a PDF

A watermark is a text or image overlaid on the pages of a document, typically semi-transparent, to indicate the document's status, ownership, or confidentiality. Common watermarks include "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", "SAMPLE", or a company name. Watermarks are a quick and effective way to communicate important information about a document's intended use.

Our free online tool lets you add a custom text watermark to all pages of any PDF in seconds.

Step 1 – Open the Add Watermark Tool

Go to our Add Watermark tool. You'll see a file upload area and a text input where you can type your watermark text.

Step 2 – Upload Your PDF and Enter Watermark Text

Upload your PDF and type the text you want to use as a watermark. Common choices include:

  • CONFIDENTIAL
  • DRAFT
  • DO NOT COPY
  • SAMPLE
  • Your company name

The watermark is applied as one line of large, light-grey, semi-transparent text at a fixed spot on each page. See the troubleshooting notes below for what that means on different page sizes.

Step 3 – Download the Watermarked PDF

After processing, download the watermarked PDF. Every page will have the watermark text applied. The watermark is embedded into the PDF, meaning it will appear when the document is opened or printed by anyone.

Tips for Using Watermarks Effectively

  • Use watermarks on drafts: Stamping "DRAFT" on a working version of a document prevents it from being mistakenly treated as a final, approved version.
  • Protect sample work: Freelancers and designers who share work samples can add "SAMPLE" or their company name to prevent the samples from being used without payment.
  • Confidential documents: For internal business documents shared outside the organisation, a "CONFIDENTIAL" watermark reminds recipients not to distribute the document further.
  • Remove before finalising: If you watermarked a draft and need the final version without a watermark, use our Remove Watermark tool or start from the original un-watermarked file.
  • Watermark before sharing: Always apply the watermark before sending the file. Once shared, you cannot remove it from the recipient's copy.

Common Uses for PDF Watermarks

Legal documents: Watermarking contracts, agreements, and legal briefs with "DRAFT" or "FOR REVIEW" signals that the document is not yet finalised and should not be acted upon.

Photography and design: Portfolios and design mockups shared with prospective clients are often watermarked to protect intellectual property while still showcasing the work.

Academic and research papers: Pre-publication versions of papers shared for peer review or feedback are commonly watermarked "DRAFT – DO NOT CITE".

Business proposals: A proposal watermarked with the company name discourages the recipient from passing it to competitors.

When the Watermark Doesn't Look Right

The end of a long watermark is cut off

The text starts 150 points (about 2 inches) in from the left edge and runs to the right in one line, so whatever does not fit before the page edge is simply lost. At the tool's text size, "DRAFT" is about 133 points wide, "DO NOT COPY" about 280 and "CONFIDENTIAL" about 293, measured with the font the tool uses. "DO NOT COPY - CONFIDENTIAL DRAFT" is about 753 points wide, and on an A4 page, which is 595 points across, it ran past the right edge in our test. Roughly 18 capital letters is the most that fits on A4 or Letter; shorter pages fit fewer. On A5 even "CONFIDENTIAL" is clipped slightly.

Short pages: the watermark moves to the middle of the page

On a page taller than about 440 points (roughly 6 inches), which covers Letter, A4 and A5, the text sits at its usual fixed spot. On a shorter page, such as a small square or a ticket-sized page, that spot would be above the edge of the page, so the tool centres the text vertically instead. In our tests it now appears on pages from 250 to 439 points tall, and the Remove Watermark tool still finds and removes it. Text that is too wide for a narrow page can still run past the right edge, as described above.

It sits in the upper left of a landscape page

The position is fixed to the page's coordinates, not to its middle. On a portrait Letter or A4 page that lands near the centre. On a landscape Letter page, our test put the text near the left, in the upper third of the page. If a centred or diagonal mark matters, this tool will not produce one.

It is almost invisible on a photo or a dark page

The mark is light grey at low opacity, and its colour cannot be changed. On a white page that reads clearly. On a dark scan or a page filled with a photograph, it can fade into the background. Look at the result on the busiest page, not only the first one.

Cyrillic or Chinese letters turn into boxes

The font only covers Latin letters. In our test, accented letters such as É and é and the € sign came out correctly, while Cyrillic and Chinese characters were drawn as solid boxes. We have not tested other scripts, so check the result before sending it if your watermark uses one.

Someone took the watermark off

A watermark made here is ordinary text laid over the page, so any PDF editor can delete it, and our own Remove Watermark tool is built to find exactly this kind of stamp. Treat it as a label, not as protection. If the document has to be locked, see Protect PDF.

The bookmarks and the document title are gone

The output is a rebuilt file. In our test, a PDF with a table of contents and a title came back with neither, while the clickable web links on the pages survived. If your file relies on its outline, plan to recreate the bookmarks after watermarking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove a watermark I've added?

You can use our Remove Watermark tool to attempt removal, or simply use the original unwatermarked file if you kept it.

Will the watermark be visible when the document is printed?

Yes. The watermark is embedded into the PDF content and will appear on screen and in print.

Can the watermark be selected, searched or copied?

Yes. It is real text, not a picture, so searching the PDF finds it and copying text from a page includes it. The page's own text underneath stays selectable as before.

Does adding the watermark change the size of my pages?

No. The page dimensions stay the same; the watermark is laid on top of the existing page.
