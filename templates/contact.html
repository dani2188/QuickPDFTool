Contact Us

Questions, bug reports, feature ideas, or privacy requests — here's how to reach us.

QuickPDFTools is built and maintained by a small team, part of the HelloBrivio ecosystem. We read every message that comes in through the address below and try to respond as quickly as we can.

📧 contact@quickpdftool.com

What to include

To help us answer faster, let us know which of these best describes your message:

  • Bug report — tell us which tool, what you uploaded (file type and roughly how large), and what went wrong. Screenshots help a lot.
  • Feature request — a tool you'd like to see, or a way an existing one could work better for your use case.
  • Privacy or data request — questions about how your files or browsing data are handled, or a request to exercise your rights under our Privacy Policy (including GDPR requests if you're in the EU/EEA).
  • Business or partnership inquiry — press, advertising, or collaboration with QuickPDFTools or another HelloBrivio product.

Before you write in

A lot of common questions are already answered without needing to wait for a reply:

  • Wondering how a specific tool works? Check its step-by-step guide — most include a full FAQ section.
  • Concerned about file privacy? See how uploaded files are handled and deleted.
  • Looking for a tool you can't find? Browse the full list of PDF tools.

Response times

We're a small team, so response times can vary, but we aim to read and reply to every message — bug reports and privacy requests are prioritised over general feedback.
