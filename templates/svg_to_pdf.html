Convert SVG to PDF Online

This free tool converts SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) files into a PDF document. Because SVG is already a vector format, the shapes, paths, and text in your file are converted directly into the PDF's own vector data — the result stays crisp at any zoom level, instead of being rasterized into a fixed-resolution image the way a JPG or PNG conversion would be.

How to convert SVG to PDF

  • 1 Upload one or more SVG files
  • 2 Click "Convert to PDF"
  • 3 Download your PDF file

Why convert SVG to PDF?

  • Sharing with anyone: Not every device or email client can preview an SVG file directly, but virtually anything can open a PDF.
  • Printing: PDF is the standard format for reliable, professional printing, while SVG rendering can vary between browsers and apps.
  • Combining multiple graphics: Upload several SVG files at once and they're combined into a single multi-page PDF, in the order you upload them.
  • Archiving designs: Icons, logos, and diagrams exported from design tools convert into a stable, self-contained document.
