WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides excellent compression while maintaining high visual quality. WebP images are significantly smaller than equivalent JPG or PNG files, making them ideal for websites where page load speed matters. Converting PDF pages to WebP produces lightweight, high-quality images that are perfect for web use.

Our free online tool converts each page of your PDF into a WebP image. No software installation required.

Step 1 – Open the PDF to WebP Tool

Go to our PDF to WebP converter. You'll see a file upload area where you can click to select your PDF or drag and drop it onto the page.

Step 2 – Upload Your PDF

Select your PDF and upload it. The tool renders each page at 2x resolution and saves each one as a WebP image with 80% quality compression — a good balance between file size and visual quality for web use.

The tool supports multi-page PDFs and processes up to 20 pages per upload.

Step 3 – Download the WebP Images

After conversion, a gallery shows all the converted images. Download each image from the list on the results page; there is no ZIP for WebP. Each image is named by its page number for easy identification.

Why Choose WebP Over JPG or PNG?

  • Smaller file sizes: WebP produces files that are typically 25–35% smaller than equivalent JPG images and up to 80% smaller than PNG, with comparable or better visual quality.
  • Faster web pages: Smaller images mean faster page loads, which improves user experience and SEO rankings.
  • Good browser support: WebP is supported by all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari (from version 14+).
  • When to use JPG instead: If you need the widest possible compatibility (including older browsers and software), JPG is more universally supported.
  • When to use PNG instead: If your images have transparent backgrounds or require pixel-perfect lossless quality, PNG is the better choice.

Common Uses for PDF to WebP Conversion

Website content: Convert PDF brochures, guides, or infographics to WebP for embedding on a website. WebP keeps your pages fast without sacrificing visual quality.

Online portfolios: Designers and photographers can convert PDF portfolio pages to WebP for display in online galleries, where lightweight images improve the browsing experience.

Blog and article illustrations: Content from PDF documents — charts, diagrams, illustrated pages — can be converted to WebP and embedded in blog posts or online articles.

E-commerce product images: Product sheets and specification PDFs can be converted to WebP for fast-loading product pages.

When Converting to WebP Doesn't Work Out

You expected a ZIP and got a list of separate downloads

The WebP result page gives one download link per page and no ZIP file, which is different from PDF to JPG. With a 20-page file that means 20 separate downloads. If you would rather have one archive and do not need WebP specifically, the JPG tool is the quicker route.

A form or program says the image format is not supported

Some upload forms, older image editors and office programs accept only JPG or PNG. The file is fine; the receiving side does not read WebP. Convert with PDF to JPG or PDF to PNG instead, and check what formats the destination takes before you convert a whole document.

The WebP files are not much smaller than JPGs would have been

How much smaller WebP gets depends on the page. Photo-heavy pages and plain text pages respond differently, and we have not measured this tool's WebP output against its JPG output. The two tools also save at different quality settings, so a size comparison between them mixes two variables. Convert one representative page both ways and compare the files before you choose a format for a whole site.

Text looks smudged or slightly blocky

WebP at this quality is lossy, and dense small text is where compression shows first. If the text is the point of the page, PDF to PNG is lossless and keeps the edges exactly.

The words and links in the page can't be used any more

Each page is turned into a picture, so text can no longer be selected or searched and links no longer click. For a web page that must stay readable to search engines, put the text in the page itself and use the image as illustration.

A page late in the document is missing

Pages after the twentieth are not converted, and the result page says how many of the total were done. To get a later page, cut it out with Split PDF first and convert the smaller file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WebP supported everywhere?

WebP is supported by all modern web browsers. For maximum compatibility with older devices, desktop applications, and non-web uses, JPG or PNG may be preferable. For websites and web apps, WebP is the best choice.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

You'll need to remove the password before converting.

Do the converted pages have a transparent background?

No. Pages are drawn onto a plain image with no transparency, so anything that was see-through in the PDF appears as it does on screen against white. If you need real transparency, WebP from this tool is not the route.

Are the images the same size as the ones from PDF to JPG?

Yes. Both tools render each page at twice its own size, so the pixel dimensions match. What differs is the format and the quality setting, and therefore the file size.
